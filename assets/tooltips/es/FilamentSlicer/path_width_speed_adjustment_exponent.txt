Speed change exponent on varying width
If single path fill is enabled, IceSL will consider 
printing tracks wider than the nozzle width. Depending 
on your printer and material, it may be necessary to 
slow down when printing larger than the nozzle. 
This exponent controls the slow down. For instance:
0.0 => no slow down
1.0 => linear, maintains constant extrusion speed
2.0 => four times slower if extruding twice the width
3.0 => eight times slower if extruding twice the width
We recommend 3.0 as a safe value.
