Flow multiplier (all)
Material flow multiplier when printing with this 
brush. Reduce or increase to artificially push more
or less plastic. Can be used to produce fragile
parts (e.g. soluble supports). This multiplier does
not apply to the tower, brim and shield. In the case
of infill, the multiplier may vary to accommodate for
lack of space. To avoid exceeding the maximal flow
rate, printing speed is decreased whenever flow
multiplier is over 1. In bridges, the flow is
additionally increased 20%.
