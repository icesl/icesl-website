Enable shield
Generates a shield structure surrounding the part. 
It serves two purposes. On single material print it 
allows to keep hot air enclosed around the part and
reduce warping. On multi-material print, it serves as
a wipe shield to catch oozing filament before it reaches
the part. 
Whether to use a shield or a tower for multi-material 
prints depends on your printer: if the printer has nozzles 
that lift up/down, or a single nozzle with multiple 
filaments, use a tower. If it is a standard setup with
multiple fixed nozzles side by side, use a shield.
