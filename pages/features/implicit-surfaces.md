Shapes from shaders, your models no longer have to look simple. Fully exploits printer resolution by avoiding tessellation.

<sup>**Left**: implicit surfaces are computed from a distance field generated from a GLSL code. **Middle**: the implicit defines a sphere perturbed by a noise. Two such spheres are used to produce a dual color result. **Right**: final print. </sup>
